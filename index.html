Where is Jhumka?

Jhumka is located in Sunsari District in eastern Nepal. A map reference places the town between Itahari and Inaruwa and describes it as roughly 7 kilometres from Itahari. A government project document also identifies Jhumka Bazaar as the core urban area of Ramdhuni Municipality's ward 5 and notes that main market activity and offices are concentrated in wards 5 and 1 within the project service area.

Jhumka and Ramdhuni Municipality

Jhumka is closely connected with Ramdhuni Municipality. The official municipal website is based at Jhumka, Sunsari, and publishes municipal notices, laws, reports, services and current public information. For administrative information, public notices, local programs and municipal services, the official municipality website should be treated as the primary source rather than this independent guide.

Official Ramdhuni Municipality website → ramdhunimun.gov.np

What is Jhumka known for?

Jhumka has a strong local-market character and serves as an urban centre within Ramdhuni Municipality. Public and map references also associate the town with local landmarks and recreational places such as Jhumka Nahar, Jhumka Park, Pipraha Playground and Miteri Pool. Some names and facilities can change over time, so a good local guide should verify the exact location, condition, opening status and visitor access before publishing detailed claims.

Jhumka's connection to nearby cities

One of the practical reasons to study Jhumka is its position in the wider Sunsari transport and settlement network. It sits between Itahari and Inaruwa, making nearby-city searches useful topics for a local guide. Route pages such as “Jhumka to Itahari” and “Jhumka to Inaruwa” should provide current map links, road information and realistic travel guidance instead of relying on outdated distances or unsupported claims.

Places to explore in and around Jhumka

A useful places directory should explain what each location is, where it is, why people visit, whether it is public or private, and what facilities are actually available. For the first version of this site, the places section can focus on Jhumka Park, Jhumka Nahar, Miteri Pool and Pipraha Playground, while gradually adding other verified destinations in Ramdhuni and nearby Sunsari.

What information should visitors check before going?

Local information changes quickly. Before travelling, visitors should confirm opening times, road conditions, entrance rules, current prices, contact numbers, public transport availability and weather. This website should link to official sources where possible and clearly mark information that has not been independently verified.

Why Jhumka Guide exists

Jhumka Guide is designed as an independent local-information resource, not as a government website and not as a directory that invents ratings or business information. The aim is to organize useful searches into practical guides: places to visit, food and cafes, schools, health facilities, banks, hotels, transportation, nearby cities and local history.
