Places to Visit in Jhumka

Jhumka is a town in Sunsari District of Koshi Province, Nepal. The area has several local landmarks, recreational places and nearby destinations that visitors can explore.

Places in Jhumka

Jhumka Park

Jhumka Park is a local recreational place and one of the recognized landmarks associated with Jhumka. It is a place visitors can explore while spending time around the town.

Jhumka Nahar

Jhumka Nahar is a local landmark associated with the Jhumka area. Visitors interested in exploring local places can include it in their visit.

Miteri Pool

Miteri Pool is a recreational landmark associated with the Jhumka area. It is one of the local places visitors may explore around Jhumka.

Pipraha Playground

Pipraha Playground is a local sports and recreational space associated with Jhumka. It is relevant for visitors looking for local recreational areas.

Selfi Pool

Selfi Pool is another local landmark associated with the Jhumka area. Visitors can explore the location as part of discovering local places around Jhumka.

Bamari / Bhamari Chowk

Bamari or Bhamari Chowk is a local area and landmark in Jhumka. It is useful as a reference point when exploring the town and surrounding roads.
