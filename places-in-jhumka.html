A Guide to Places in Jhumka

Jhumka is a town in Sunsari District of Koshi Province, Nepal. It lies between Itahari and Inaruwa and is associated with Ramdhuni. Current map and reference sources identify several local landmarks in and around Jhumka.

This page organizes the main places into two groups: locations associated directly with Jhumka and destinations that can be reached from Jhumka. This distinction helps visitors understand the area without confusing nearby destinations with places physically inside Jhumka.

Planning a visit: Information such as access, opening hours, facilities, road conditions and public transport can change. Verify time-sensitive information before travelling.
