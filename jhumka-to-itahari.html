Jhumka to Itahari Distance

The road distance from Jhumka to Itahari is approximately 9 km. A published route estimate gives about 9 km of driving distance and around 9–10 minutes of driving time in favorable conditions. Your actual distance can vary depending on the exact point where you start in Jhumka and where you finish in Itahari.

Jhumka to Itahari Route

The normal road connection is toward Itahari using the Mahendra Highway (East–West Highway / H01). Jhumka is on the Mahendra Highway corridor in Sunsari, and Itahari is a major road junction in the same corridor.

For a live route, traffic information and your exact starting point, use the Google Maps directions button above. Google Maps can automatically adjust the route when traffic, construction or other road conditions change.

Jhumka to Itahari by Bus

Local public transport is the practical low-cost option for this short trip. Jhumka has a western-side bus stand on the Mahendra Highway, and Itahari has an established bus park. Local buses and other public vehicles may not follow a fixed online timetable, so departure frequency can change during the day.

Estimated bus/local-public-transport time: about 20–35 minutes, depending on stops, traffic and waiting time.

For the latest departure, ask at the Jhumka bus stand for a vehicle going toward Itahari and confirm the fare before boarding. This page does not publish a fixed fare because a current route-specific fare was not reliably available online.
