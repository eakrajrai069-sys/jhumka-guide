About Jhumka

Jhumka, Sunsari

Jhumka is a town in Sunsari District of Koshi Province, Nepal. It is associated with Ramdhuni Municipality and is connected with nearby urban areas including Itahari and Inaruwa.

About this website: This website is an independent local guide designed to organize useful information about Jhumka in one place. Business details, opening hours, transport information and other time-sensitive information should be verified before visiting.
